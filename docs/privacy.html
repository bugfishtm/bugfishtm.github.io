2. General Information

This is a purely static, non-commercial website. It consists of a start page that forwards you to our main website bugfish.eu, this privacy policy and the Impressum; addresses that do not exist (for example outdated links) forward you the same way. It does not use cookies, does not use analytics or tracking tools, does not embed third-party content (the stylesheet, the forwarding script and the icon are served from this website itself; texts are shown in the fonts installed on your device), and does not contain contact forms, comment functions or any other input fields that transmit data to us.

Personal data is therefore only processed to the technically necessary extent described below.

3. Hosting via GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA ("GitHub").

When you visit this website, GitHub automatically processes technical data that your browser transmits, in particular:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • Date and time of the request
  • Requested page / file (URL)
  • Browser type and version, operating system (user agent)
  • Referrer URL

This data is processed by GitHub in server log files for the purpose of delivering the website, ensuring its stability and security, and defending against attacks. We ourselves have no access to these log files.

The legal basis for this processing is Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the secure and reliable provision of this website.

GitHub may transfer this data to servers in the United States. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of data protection (Art. 45 GDPR).

Further information can be found in GitHub's privacy documentation: GitHub General Privacy Statement and About GitHub Pages - data collection.

4. Forwarding to bugfish.eu

The start page of this website, as well as any address on this website that does not exist (for example an outdated link), automatically forwards your browser to https://bugfish.eu after five seconds. A small script shows the countdown and starts the forwarding. It runs only in your browser, stores nothing on your device and sends no data to us. If JavaScript is disabled, your browser performs the same forwarding by itself.

When you are forwarded, or click the link to bugfish.eu, your browser connects to the servers of bugfish.eu. Like every web server, they receive the technical data your browser transmits (see section 3, in particular your IP address), and your browser usually tells them the address of this website as the referrer. From that moment on, the privacy policy published on bugfish.eu applies.

The forwarding serves our legitimate interest in directing visitors to our main website (Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR). If you do not want to be forwarded, open the Impressum or this privacy policy via the links in the footer before the countdown ends.

5. No Cookies, No Tracking

This website does not set any cookies, does not store anything in your browser's local storage, and does not use web analytics, advertising networks, social media plugins, or fingerprinting technologies.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in answering your enquiry) or, if your enquiry is aimed at concluding a contract, Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR. Your data will be deleted once the correspondence is complete, unless statutory retention obligations apply.
